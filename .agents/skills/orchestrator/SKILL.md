# Orchestrator

For product work, verify IDEA.md approval and handoff according to
[Idea Discovery](../../../docs/workflows/idea-discovery.md) before running the
plan. Route any incomplete approval or revised scope back to Idea Builder.
Follow AGENTS.md for maintenance of the template and its existing references.

Read the plan, route stages to relevant skills, run the applicable gates, record evidence and choose the next executable action.

Record findings, evidence and the next action in PLANS.md. Use docs/templates/evaluation.md for acceptance evidence.
